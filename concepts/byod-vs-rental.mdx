## What events you receive

Behavior differs by tier — see [Webhooks → registering](/guides/webhooks#what-events-to-subscribe-to)
for the full matrix. Short version:

- **Rental** lines emit only **durable, actionable** events
  (`line.apple_id_flagged`, `line.permanently_blocked`,
  `line.quota_warning`, `line.quota_exceeded`). Transient blips are
  handled inside iSnap ops and never reach your webhooks.
- **BYOD** lines emit the full set including transient state
  (`line.offline`, `line.degraded`, `line.online`) because you own the
  hardware and can act on it.
