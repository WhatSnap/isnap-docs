title: "Pre-orders"
description: "Order one rented iMessage line in the area codes you prefer, confirm the order, pay its first month as a direct account, and receive the line when iSnap hands it over."
---

A pre-order asks iSnap to prepare one rented iMessage line (`billing_tier: rental_iphone`) for your account, preferably with a number in one of up to 5 area codes that you choose. It has two steps: a draft, which has no effect, then a confirmation, which commits the order. A direct account then pays the first month in the iSnap dashboard before iSnap builds the line. Read this page when you order a line instead of taking one from the [marketplace](/guides/getting-a-line).

One pre-order is one line. To order several lines, create several pre-orders.

## How a pre-order works

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose area codes">

Call [`GET /v1/area-codes`](/api-reference/pre-orders/list-area-codes). It needs no credential and returns the whole list in one response:

- `area_codes`: each entry has `area_code`, `region`, `country_code`, `purchase_zip`, `alt_zips` and `shared_with`. The list is ordered by `country_code`, then by `area_code`.
- `countries`: each entry has `country_code`, `name` and `calling_code`.

The query parameter `country` takes 2 letters, case-insensitive, and keeps the area codes of that country. A country with no area code returns an empty `area_codes`. `countries` is never filtered.

The list contains the area codes that you can request on a pre-order. It does not say which lines are in stock: the [marketplace](/guides/getting-a-line) does. When `shared_with` is not empty, it names the other area codes of the list that cover the same geography, and the number that you obtain can carry any of them. `purchase_zip`, which can be `null`, and `alt_zips` are ZIP codes of that geography.

</Step>

<Step title="Create a draft">

Call [`POST /v1/pre-orders`](/api-reference/pre-orders/create-pre-order). Every field of the body is optional:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `preferred_area_codes` | 0 to 5 area codes, in your order of preference. Each is a string of 1 to 10 characters. The default is `[]`: any area code. The API does not check the values against `GET /v1/area-codes`. |
| `inbound_only` | `false`, the default, orders a two-way line. `true` orders an inbound-only line. See [Line kinds](/concepts#line-kinds). |
| `billing_anchor_at` | The [billing anchor](/guides/managing-lines#anniversary-and-billing-anchor) to give the line. A timestamp more than 31 days after the call returns `400 billing_anchor_too_far`. The pre-order returns it, and the line receives it when the build starts. |
| `billing_tier` | Leave it out. The only accepted value is `rental_iphone`, and the pre-order neither stores nor returns it. |
| `metadata` | Your own keys and values: at most 20 keys and 8,192 bytes once serialized. |

Any other field, `quantity` included, returns `400 validation_failed`.

The response is `201` with the pre-order, `status: draft`. A draft creates no line, takes no payment and starts nothing at iSnap. A draft never expires, and the API sets no maximum number of drafts.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm">

Call [`POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/confirm`](/api-reference/pre-orders/confirm-pre-order). Only a draft can be confirmed, and a direct account can repeat the confirmation while the first month is due. The build of the line starts with the confirmation for a wholesale partner, and once the first month is paid for a direct account: see [Confirm and payment](#confirm-and-payment). When the build starts:

- The pre-order gets `status: provisioning`.
- `estimated_fulfillment` is set to the time the build starts plus 72 hours.
- iSnap creates a line on your account with `status: provisioning`: a rented iMessage line (`billing_tier: rental_iphone`), with the `inbound_only` that you ordered and your `billing_anchor_at`. When a number is in stock in one of your area codes, in their order of preference, the line receives it when the build starts. A pre-order with no `preferred_area_codes` takes any number in stock.

</Step>

<Step title="Receive the line">

When iSnap hands the line over, the pre-order becomes `fulfilled` and iSnap sends `pre_order.fulfilled` once, with `pre_order_id`, `line_id`, `phone_number` (the full number of the line, which can be `null`), `billing_tier` and `fulfilled_at`. The pre-order itself carries no line id: you learn the line from this event, or by reading your lines with [`GET /v1/lines/my`](/api-reference/lines/list-my-lines). A line-scoped key does not see the new line in `GET /v1/lines/my`, since the line is not in its list: read the line id from the event, or list with a key that has no list of lines.

A direct account also receives an email that says the line is ready. A wholesale partner receives no email.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Confirm and payment

**Wholesale partner.** The build starts with the confirmation: the response has `status: provisioning`, and `stripe_client_secret` is `null`. Nothing is paid at confirmation. The line is counted on your monthly invoice from the day it goes live.

**Direct account.** The build starts once the first month is paid. The confirmation starts the monthly billing of the line and returns the pre-order with `status: awaiting_payment` and `stripe_client_secret`, the secret of the first monthly payment: do not log it. No line exists yet, and `estimated_fulfillment` is `null`.

- Pay the first month in the [iSnap dashboard](https://platform.isnap.ai), by card, within 23 hours of the confirmation. No operation that an API key can call pays it. See [Accounts](/concepts#accounts).
- Once iSnap has recorded the payment, the build starts. Read the pre-order with [`GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/get-pre-order) until `status` is `provisioning`: that read checks the payment, and starts the build when the payment has succeeded. [`GET /v1/pre-orders`](/api-reference/pre-orders/list-pre-orders) does not check it: see [Listing](#listing).
- When the first month is not paid within 23 hours of the confirmation, it can no longer be paid: the pre-order becomes `cancelled`, no line is built, and iSnap sends `pre_order.cancelled`. The API does not bound the delay between the end of the 23 hours and that change. To order the line again, create a new draft.
- The first month starts at the confirmation, not at the hand-over. The hand-over takes no payment.
- The confirmation can also return `status: provisioning` and `stripe_client_secret: null` to a direct account: its first month was already paid, and the build has started.

A direct account can repeat the confirmation while the pre-order is `awaiting_payment`: it returns the same `stripe_client_secret` and starts no second billing. Repeated after the payment succeeded, it returns the pre-order in `provisioning`, with `stripe_client_secret: null`. A wholesale partner cannot repeat a confirmation: `400 pre_order_not_draft`.

<Warning>
`stripe_client_secret` is returned only by the `confirm` response of a pre-order in `awaiting_payment`: every other response returns `null`. `confirm` does not honor `Idempotency-Key`.

Once its build has started, a pre-order cannot be canceled through the API. Confirm only when the order is final.
</Warning>

When the response to a confirmation is lost, or the call returns a `500` or a `503`, read the pre-order with [`GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/get-pre-order) before you do anything else:

- `status: draft`: the confirmation did not happen. Resend it.
- `status: awaiting_payment`: the confirmation happened, and the first month is due. Pay it in the dashboard. Resending the confirmation returns the same `stripe_client_secret`.
- `status: provisioning`: the build has started, and nothing is due.
- `status: cancelled`: the pre-order was canceled, or its first month was not paid within 23 hours. Create a new draft to order the line again.

Two refusals concern the payment of the first month, on the confirmation and on the cancellation. `503 payment_provider_unavailable`: iSnap could not reach its payment provider, nothing changed, and the call can be resent. `409 payment_setup_failed`: the payment provider refused the request for your account, nothing changed, and resending the call does not help. Write to [support@isnap.ai](mailto:support@isnap.ai) with the `trace_id` and `request_id` of the response.

A confirmation acts the same way with every API key:

- **Test keys.** An `isnap_test_` account key is not a separate test environment: it acts on the same account, lines and billing as an `isnap_live_` key. A confirmation sent with a test key is a real order, billed like any other. See [Test keys](/authentication#test-keys).
- **Line-scoped keys.** The list of lines of a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) is not applied to pre-orders: such a key can create and confirm a pre-order.

### Create a draft and confirm it

<CodeGroup>

```bash curl
# No credential needed.
curl -sS "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/area-codes?country=US"

curl -sS -X POST "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/pre-orders" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"preferred_area_codes":["415","628"],"inbound_only":false,"metadata":{"crm_account_id":"acct_1042"}}'

# The response is saved before you read it. For a direct account it has status awaiting_payment and
# contains stripe_client_secret: keep the file out of logs, and delete it once read.
curl -sS -X POST "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/pre-orders/7f3c9a52-1d4e-4b8a-9c6f-2e5d8a1b4c7d/confirm" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY" \
  -o confirm-response.json
```

```typescript TypeScript
import { APIError, ISnapClient } from '@isnap/sdk'

const client = new ISnapClient({ apiKey: process.env.ISNAP_API_KEY ?? '', maxRetries: 2 })

// Neither call honors Idempotency-Key, so the SDK must not retry them on its own.
const once = { maxRetries: 0 }

const draft = await client.preOrders.create(
  {
    preferred_area_codes: ['415', '628'],
    inbound_only: false,
    metadata: { crm_account_id: 'acct_1042' }
  },
  once
)
console.log(draft.id, draft.status)

try {
  const confirmed = await client.preOrders.confirm(draft.id, once)
  // For a direct account whose first month is due, confirmed.stripe_client_secret is the
  // secret of the first monthly payment: do not log it.
  if (confirmed.stripe_client_secret !== null) {
    console.log('Pay the first month in the iSnap dashboard within 23 hours.')
  } else {
    console.log(confirmed.status, confirmed.estimated_fulfillment)
  }
} catch (err) {
  if (err instanceof APIError && err.code === 'pre_order_not_draft') {
    // Already confirmed or canceled: read it with preOrders.get.
    console.error('This pre-order is not a draft.')
  } else {
    throw err
  }
}
```

```python Python
import os

import requests

API = "https://api.isnap.ai"
HEADERS = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['ISNAP_API_KEY']}"}

# Neither call honors Idempotency-Key: do not retry them blindly.
created = requests.post(
    f"{API}/v1/pre-orders",
    headers=HEADERS,
    json={
        "preferred_area_codes": ["415", "628"],
        "inbound_only": False,
        "metadata": {"crm_account_id": "acct_1042"},
    },
    timeout=30,
)
created.raise_for_status()
draft = created.json()["data"]
print(draft["id"], draft["status"])

confirmed = requests.post(f"{API}/v1/pre-orders/{draft['id']}/confirm", headers=HEADERS, timeout=30)
payload = confirmed.json()

if confirmed.status_code == 200:
    pre_order = payload["data"]
    # For a direct account whose first month is due, pre_order["stripe_client_secret"] is the
    # secret of the first monthly payment: do not log it.
    if pre_order["stripe_client_secret"] is not None:
        print("Pay the first month in the iSnap dashboard within 23 hours.")
    else:
        print(pre_order["status"], pre_order["estimated_fulfillment"])
elif payload["error"]["code"] == "pre_order_not_draft":
    # Already confirmed or canceled: read it with GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}.
    print("This pre-order is not a draft.")
else:
    confirmed.raise_for_status()
```

## Estimated date

`estimated_fulfillment` is `null` until the build starts: on a draft, on a pre-order in `awaiting_payment`, and on a pre-order canceled before its build started. The start of the build sets it once, to that time plus 72 hours, and nothing revises it afterward. The 72 hours are a setting of iSnap, the same for every pre-order. No field and no event tells you that the date has passed without a hand-over.

## Statuses

| `status` | Meaning | Set by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `draft` | The pre-order has no effect. | `POST /v1/pre-orders` |
| `awaiting_payment` | A direct account confirmed the pre-order, and its first month is due. Nothing is built until it is paid. | `POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/confirm`, for a direct account. |
| `provisioning` | The build has started, and iSnap prepares the line. | The confirmation of a wholesale partner, or the payment of the first month of a direct account. |
| `fulfilled` | iSnap handed the line over to you. | iSnap, at the hand-over. |
| `cancelled` | The pre-order ended with no line: you canceled it, its first month was not paid within 23 hours of the confirmation, or the payment of its first month was canceled before it was made. | `POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/cancel`, or iSnap. |

A pre-order whose build has started never becomes `cancelled`. If iSnap withdraws the line before the hand-over, the pre-order stays `provisioning` and no event is sent.

`device_status` tells how far the line is. It is always present and never `null`:

| `device_status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `awaiting_hardware` | The pre-order has no line: it is a draft, in `awaiting_payment` or canceled. Or the line is not finished. |
| `ready` | The line is built. |
| `deployed` | The line is handed over. |

The schema also lists `received` and `setup_in_progress`. The API does not return them. Ignore response fields, enum values and event types you do not know.

## Canceling

[`POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/cancel`](/api-reference/pre-orders/cancel-pre-order) cancels a pre-order in `draft` or `awaiting_payment`: the pre-order gets `status: cancelled`, nothing was charged, and iSnap sends `pre_order.cancelled`. The event is sent after the cancel is recorded: if it cannot be sent, the cancel stands.

- For a pre-order in `awaiting_payment`, the payment of the first month is canceled first: the `stripe_client_secret` of the confirmation can no longer be used.
- When the first month is already paid, the call returns `409 pre_order_already_paid` and the build goes on.
- A pre-order in `provisioning`, `fulfilled` or `cancelled` returns `400 pre_order_already_confirmed`.
- `503 payment_provider_unavailable` and `409 payment_setup_failed` mean what they mean for the confirmation: see [Confirm and payment](#confirm-and-payment).

No operation ends a pre-order whose build has started. The message of the `400` above points to `POST /v1/lines/{id}/release`, but release only gives back a line that you have reserved: it returns `404` for a line that is being prepared. `POST /v1/lines/{id}/terminate` refuses that line too. To withdraw such a pre-order, write to [support@isnap.ai](mailto:support@isnap.ai) with the `id` of the pre-order.

## Metadata

Set `metadata` when you create the draft, and change it with [`PATCH /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/update-pre-order). The body of the `PATCH` is required and has one field, `metadata`. Send `Content-Type: application/json` with every body.

- The `PATCH` merges: a key that you send replaces the stored value, a key sent as `null` is deleted, and the other keys stay. `{"metadata": {}}` changes nothing.
- The `metadata` of the request and the result each contain at most 20 keys and 8,192 bytes once serialized. A request that only deletes keys is never refused for the size of the result.
- The `PATCH` works in every status.
- The events sent after the `PATCH` carry the new `metadata` in `data.metadata`.
- The `metadata` of a pre-order is not copied to the line that it produces.

## Listing

[`GET /v1/pre-orders`](/api-reference/pre-orders/list-pre-orders) lists your pre-orders, newest first.

| Parameter | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `status` | Keeps one status: `draft`, `awaiting_payment`, `provisioning`, `fulfilled` or `cancelled`. |
| `limit` | 1 to 100. The default is 25. A value outside the range returns `400 validation_failed`. |
| `cursor` | The `next_cursor` of the previous page. |

The response has `data.items`, `data.has_more` and `data.next_cursor`. `next_cursor` is `null` on the last page.

The list does not check payments: a pre-order whose first month was just paid can be listed as `awaiting_payment` until iSnap records the payment. [`GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/get-pre-order) checks it.

Two rules of the cursor, described with the other lists in [Cursors](/pagination#cursors):

- A cursor that cannot be read does not return an error: the list starts again from the first page, with `200`.
- The cursor does not carry the `status` filter. Send the same `status` on every page of one walk, or pre-orders are skipped.

<CodeGroup>

```bash curl
curl -sS "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/pre-orders?status=provisioning&limit=100" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY"
```

```typescript TypeScript
import { ISnapClient } from '@isnap/sdk'

const client = new ISnapClient({ apiKey: process.env.ISNAP_API_KEY ?? '', maxRetries: 2 })

// preOrders.list returns one page: pass next_cursor back as cursor, with the same status.
let cursor: string | undefined
do {
  const page = await client.preOrders.list({ status: 'provisioning', limit: 100, cursor })
  for (const preOrder of page.items) {
    console.log(preOrder.id, preOrder.device_status, preOrder.estimated_fulfillment)
  }
  cursor = page.has_more && page.next_cursor ? page.next_cursor : undefined
} while (cursor)
```

```python Python
import os

import requests

params = {"status": "provisioning", "limit": 100}

while True:
    response = requests.get(
        "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/pre-orders",
        headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['ISNAP_API_KEY']}"},
        params=params,
        timeout=30,
    )
    response.raise_for_status()
    page = response.json()["data"]
    for pre_order in page["items"]:
        print(pre_order["id"], pre_order["device_status"], pre_order["estimated_fulfillment"])
    if not page["has_more"] or page["next_cursor"] is None:
        break
    # Keep the same status on every page of the walk.
    params["cursor"] = page["next_cursor"]
```

</CodeGroup>

## Events

| Event | Sent when | Line filter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`pre_order.fulfilled`](/webhooks/events/pre-order#pre_order-fulfilled) | The line of a pre-order is handed over. | Tied to the new line: a subscription with a line filter receives it only when the filter lists that line. |
| [`pre_order.cancelled`](/webhooks/events/pre-order#pre_order-cancelled) | You cancel a pre-order in `draft` or `awaiting_payment`, its first month is not paid within 23 hours of the confirmation, or the payment of its first month is canceled before it is made. | Tied to no line: a subscription with a line filter never receives it. |

A pre-order does not give you its line id before the hand-over: receive pre-order events on a [webhook subscription](/webhooks/overview) without a line filter. When the line is handed over with its device already connected, iSnap also sends `line.connected` for it.

The pre-order events announce the two changes above, and no other. No event announces:

- a draft that is created;
- a confirmation: its response is the only signal;
- a first month that is paid, and the start of the build: read the pre-order;
- a change of `metadata`;
- an `estimated_fulfillment` that passes;
- an order that iSnap withdraws behind a pre-order whose build has started.

The full list is in [What events do not announce](/webhooks/event-catalog#what-events-do-not-announce). To know the state of a pre-order, read it with [`GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/get-pre-order). Deduplicate on `event_id`: see [Duplicates](/webhooks/delivery#duplicates).

## Errors

The refusals of each operation are on its reference page: [`GET /v1/area-codes`](/api-reference/pre-orders/list-area-codes), [`POST /v1/pre-orders`](/api-reference/pre-orders/create-pre-order), [`GET /v1/pre-orders`](/api-reference/pre-orders/list-pre-orders), [`GET /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/get-pre-order), [`PATCH /v1/pre-orders/{id}`](/api-reference/pre-orders/update-pre-order), [`POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/confirm`](/api-reference/pre-orders/confirm-pre-order) and [`POST /v1/pre-orders/{id}/cancel`](/api-reference/pre-orders/cancel-pre-order). The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).

## Personal data

A pre-order carries no data beyond what you put in its `metadata`. `pre_order.fulfilled` carries the full phone number of the new line. Do not copy it into logs, tickets or other tools.
