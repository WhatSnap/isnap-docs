---
title: "List area codes"
description: "Lists the area codes that can be requested on a pre-order, and the countries they belong to."
openapi: "GET /v1/area-codes"
---

- **Credential:** none.
- **Events:** none.
- **Pages:** none. The whole list is returned in one response. `area_codes` is ordered by `country_code`, then by `area_code`, and `countries` by `country_code`.
- **Meaning:** the list contains the area codes that a pre-order can request. It does not say which lines are in stock, and a pre-order does not check its `preferred_area_codes` against it. See [How a pre-order works](/guides/pre-orders#how-a-pre-order-works).

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `validation_failed` | `country` is not 2 letters. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
