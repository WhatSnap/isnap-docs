---
title: "Update the metadata of a hosted macOS session"
description: "Merges metadata into a hosted macOS session that your account rents."
openapi: "PATCH /v1/macos-sessions/{id}"
---

- **Credential:** an API key of the account that rents the session. Every other account receives `404 macos_session_not_found`, never `403`. See [Who can rent through the API](/guides/hosted-macos-sessions#who-can-rent-through-the-api).
- **Line-scoped keys:** the list of lines of a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) is not applied.
- **`Idempotency-Key`:** not honored. See [Operations that honor the key](/idempotency#operations-that-honor-the-key).
- **Events:** none.
- **Body:** required. It has one field, `metadata`, and is sent with `Content-Type: application/json`. The merge rules are in [Metadata](/guides/hosted-macos-sessions#metadata).

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `validation_failed` | `id` is not `mcs_` followed by 25 letters or digits, the body has an unknown field, or its `metadata` is over 20 keys or 8,192 bytes. |
| 400 | `metadata_too_large` | The merged `metadata` is over 20 keys or 8,192 bytes. |
| 404 | `macos_session_not_found` | The session is unknown, released, or rented by another account. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
