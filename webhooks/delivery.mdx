This page states what iSnap guarantees when it delivers an event to your endpoint, and what it does
not. Read it before you decide how your receiver deduplicates, orders and recovers events. Each rule
is stated here once: the other pages link to it.

## What is guaranteed

- **At least once, while the subscription stays active.** iSnap sends a recorded event to each
  subscription it matched until your endpoint returns `2xx` or `410`, or the attempts run out. A
  delivery can end before its attempts run out: when the subscription is turned off or deleted, when
  its URL is refused, when iSnap would resend it after a suspension or a stall more than 24 hours
  after its creation, and when it waits or stalls again after iSnap resent it 3 times.
  See [Success and failure](#success-and-failure) and [Suspension](#suspension). A delivery that ends
  `failed` can be [replayed](#replays) while its record exists, and its event read from the
  [events feed](/webhooks/pulling-events). An event can be delivered more than once.
- **An event is recorded only if it matches an active subscription when it occurs.** An event that
  matches no active subscription at that moment is never delivered and never listed.
- **An event can be lost**: see
  [What events do not announce](/webhooks/event-catalog#what-events-do-not-announce).
- **No delay is promised.** The API does not bound the delay between a change and the first attempt.

The resource operations are the source of truth. Use events to act fast, and reconcile from the
API: see [What events do not announce](/webhooks/event-catalog#what-events-do-not-announce).

## Duplicates

Deduplicate on `event_id`. It is the same value as the `webhook-id` header, and it is the same on
every attempt, on a replay, and for every subscription that receives the event.

The same event reaches you more than once when:

- your endpoint processed a request but returned its response after 10 seconds;
- iSnap resends a delivery after a suspension ends, or after it recovers a delivery that stalled;
- you replay a delivery;
- two of your subscriptions have the same URL and both match the event;
- a line that is not a BYOD line stays offline: its durable
  [`line.offline`](/webhooks/events/line#line-offline) is delivered again with the same `event_id`.

Never deduplicate on a hash of the body. The first attempt and its scheduled retries send the same
bytes, but a replay, or a delivery that iSnap resends after a suspension or a stall, can order the
JSON keys differently. The content is the same, and the signature covers the bytes of each request.

## Order

Delivery order is not guaranteed. Order events by the timestamps inside `data`, not by arrival. For
message events, apply
[Building the status from events](/guides/sending-messages#building-the-status-from-events).

Deliveries are sent concurrently and retried independently, so events can arrive in any order, two
events about the same message included. The `created_at` of the envelope is the time iSnap recorded
the event, not the time the change happened.

## Success and failure

An attempt succeeds when your endpoint returns a status from `200` to `299` within 10 seconds. iSnap
does not interpret the response body, but it keeps its first 1,024 characters in the
[delivery record](#delivery-records), readable with any API key of your account: return an empty
body.

Every other outcome is a failed attempt:

- a `3xx` status: iSnap never follows a redirect;
- a `4xx` status, `400`, `401`, `404` and `429` included;
- a `5xx` status;
- no response within 10 seconds;
- a host that does not resolve, a connection error, or a TLS error.

A failed attempt is retried, except after a `410`. iSnap ignores a `Retry-After` header.

Four outcomes end a delivery without another attempt:

| Outcome | Delivery `status` | Effect on the subscription |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your endpoint returns `2xx` within 10 seconds. | `success` | `failure_count` returns to 0. |
| Your endpoint returns `410`. | `failed` | It becomes inactive: see [A 410 response](#a-410-response). |
| The URL is refused when the attempt is about to be sent, because it points to a non-public address. | `failed`, no request sent | It is suspended immediately: see [Suspension](#suspension). |
| The subscription is inactive. | `failed`, no request sent | None. |

## Retries

A retry schedule has at most 7 attempts. Each delay is counted from the previous attempt. A delivery
that iSnap resends after a suspension or a stall starts a new schedule, so one delivery can have more
than 7 attempts in all.

| Attempt | Delay after the previous attempt |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | None |
| 2 | 30 seconds |
| 3 | 1 minute |
| 4 | 5 minutes |
| 5 | 30 minutes |
| 6 | 2 hours |
| 7 | 12 hours |

The delays add up to 14 hours 36 minutes 30 seconds, so the 7th attempt starts at least that long
after the first.

- Every attempt carries the same `webhook-id` and a new `webhook-timestamp` and signature.
- A new `url` set with `PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}` applies to every request not sent before the change,
  pending retries included.
- When the 7th attempt fails, the delivery ends `failed` and the `failure_count` of the subscription
  rises by 1.
- A delivery that stays `pending` with no attempt scheduled is resent by iSnap, with a full new
  schedule, if it was created less than 24 hours earlier. Past 24 hours it ends `failed`. It can
  also end `failed` when it stalls again after iSnap resent it 3 times. In both cases its
  `response_body_snippet` starts with `abandoned:`, and you can replay it while its record exists.

An outage of your endpoint shorter than 14 hours 36 minutes 30 seconds does not by itself suspend the
subscription: the deliveries created during the outage keep their remaining attempts, on the
schedule above. Read the [events feed](/webhooks/pulling-events) to catch up without waiting for
them.

## Suspension

iSnap suspends a subscription in two cases:

| Cause | When |
| --- | --- |
| `failure_count` reaches 10. | `failure_count` is the number of consecutive deliveries that used all their attempts without a `2xx`. Each of these deliveries first runs its full retry schedule. |
| The URL is refused when an attempt is about to be sent, because it points to a non-public address. | Immediately. `failure_count` does not change. |

`failure_count` counts deliveries, not attempts. One successful delivery, or one successful probe,
resets it to 0. `last_success_at` is the time of the last `2xx`, from a delivery or a probe.
`last_failure_at` is the time of the last delivery that used all its attempts, the last `410`, or
the last refused URL. Both are `null` until the first such outcome. These three fields are returned
by [`POST /v1/webhooks`](/api-reference/webhooks/create-webhook) and
[`GET /v1/webhooks`](/api-reference/webhooks/list-webhooks) only.

### While a subscription is suspended

- A delivery that was already created waits: no request is sent, its `status` stays `pending`, and
  its `next_retry_at` becomes `null`.
- iSnap sends a [probe](/webhooks/events/webhook#webhook-test): a signed `webhook.test` request. Two
  probes are at least 15 minutes apart. The first is sent within 15 minutes of the suspension,
  unless the subscription was already probed in the 15 minutes before it.
- A subscription whose URL is refused is not probed until the URL passes the check again: after you
  change it with `PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}`, or after its host resolves to a public address again.

<Warning>
  The events that occur while a subscription is suspended are lost for it. They are not recorded,
  they are not delivered after it resumes, they cannot be replayed, and they are absent from the
  events feed unless another active subscription of your account matched them. After a suspension,
  reconcile from the resource operations.
</Warning>

### How a suspension ends

A `2xx` response to a probe, received within 10 seconds, resumes the subscription: `failure_count`
returns to 0 and `last_success_at` is set. Any other response, a redirect, a timeout or a network
error leaves it suspended.

No operation resumes a suspended subscription. `PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}` with `active: true`, and a
change of URL, do not end a suspension: only a successful probe does.

After the subscription resumes, iSnap resends each delivery that waited, with a full new retry
schedule that starts at attempt 1. The API does not bound the delay between the resume and the
resend. There are two exceptions:

- a delivery created more than 24 hours before the time iSnap would resend it ends `failed`, with
  `response_body_snippet` set to `abandoned: older than 24h`;
- a delivery that iSnap already resent 3 times can end `failed` instead, with
  `response_body_snippet` set to `abandoned: rescued 3 times`.

You can replay such a delivery while its record exists. The events that occurred during the
suspension are not sent.

### How to detect a suspension

No field says that a subscription is suspended, and no event announces it. A suspended subscription
still reads `active: true`. Use these signs:

| Sign | Where you see it |
| --- | --- |
| Your endpoint receives a `webhook.test` request, with the header `X-iSnap-Probe: 1`. | In your receiver. A probe is sent only to a suspended subscription. |
| `failure_count` is 10 or more. | [`GET /v1/webhooks`](/api-reference/webhooks/list-webhooks). A suspension for a refused URL does not move this counter. |
| Deliveries stay `pending` with `next_retry_at: null`. | [`GET /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries`](/api-reference/webhooks/list-webhook-deliveries) with `status=pending`. |

To avoid a suspension, monitor the failures of your endpoint, and check that the host of your URL
keeps resolving to a public address.

## A 410 response

A `410` response to a delivery ends that delivery `failed` and sets the subscription to
`active: false` immediately. It does not change `failure_count`.

An inactive subscription is not probed. Turn it on again with
[`PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}`](/api-reference/webhooks/update-webhook) and `active: true`. The events
that occurred while it was inactive are lost for it.

A `410` response to a probe has no special effect: the subscription stays suspended.

## Turning off and changing a subscription

[`PATCH /v1/webhooks/{id}`](/api-reference/webhooks/update-webhook) accepts any subset of `url`,
`events`, `line_ids`, `description` and `active`. A body with none of them returns
`400 validation_failed`.

| Field | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `active: false` | Stops new deliveries. A delivery that was already created ends `failed` at its next attempt, without a request, with `response_body_snippet` set to `not sent: subscription inactive`. It does not count in `failure_count`. Replay it after you set `active: true`: a replay sent while the subscription is inactive ends `failed` the same way. |
| `active: true` | Resumes the subscription for the events that occur afterward. Nothing that occurred in between is sent. It does not end a [suspension](#suspension). |
| `url` | The new URL passes the same checks as on create, and applies to every request not sent before the change, pending retries included. |
| `events` | Replaces the whole event filter. The new filter applies to the events recorded afterward: the deliveries that already exist are still sent. |
| `line_ids` | An array replaces the line filter. `null` removes it. |
| `description` | A text of 255 characters at most, or `null`. |

## Replays

[`POST /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries/{delivery_id}/replay`](/api-reference/webhooks/replay-webhook-delivery)
creates a new delivery of the event of a finished delivery. A replay targets a delivery of a
subscription, not an event id.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Which deliveries | Only a delivery whose `status` is `success` or `failed`. A `pending` delivery returns `422 delivery_not_terminal`. |
| Result | The call returns `202` with `delivery_id`, `replay_of` and `status: "pending"`. The new delivery has a new id, the same `event_id` and the same content. |
| How it is sent | To the URL the subscription has now, signed with the secret it has now, on the retry schedule above, starting at attempt 1. If it uses all its attempts, `failure_count` rises by 1. |
| How long | While the record of the delivery exists: at least 72 hours after its creation, and only while its subscription exists. After that the call returns `404 not_found`. |
| Subscription state | A replay is accepted whatever the state of the subscription. For an inactive subscription the new delivery ends `failed` without a request. For a suspended subscription it waits, like any other delivery: see [How a suspension ends](#how-a-suspension-ends). |
| Limits | The API sets no limit on the number of replays of one delivery. A replay can itself be replayed. |
| `503 replay_enqueue_failed` | iSnap could not queue the replay. The new delivery exists with status `failed`: call again. |

A replay records a new delivery of the event at the time of the replay. The events feed lists an
event at the time of its earliest delivery inside your window: while the first delivery is inside the
window, a replay does not move the event; otherwise the event is listed at the time of the replay,
with its original `created_at`. Deduplicate on `event_id`.

The sample replays every event of the last 24 hours whose latest delivery failed. The list returns
the newest record first, so the first record seen for an `event_id` is its latest delivery.

<CodeGroup>

```bash curl
# List the failed deliveries of the last 24 hours.
SINCE=$(date -u -v-24H +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ 2>/dev/null || date -u -d '24 hours ago' +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ)
curl -sS -G "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/webhooks/wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6/deliveries" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY" \
  --data-urlencode "status=failed" \
  --data-urlencode "since=$SINCE" \
  --data-urlencode "limit=100"

# Replay one of them.
curl -sS -X POST \
  "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/webhooks/wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6/deliveries/whd_8Vc3Tq6Lx9Hn2Kb5Wm7Zd4Ry1/replay" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY"
```

```typescript TypeScript
import { APIError, ISnapClient } from '@isnap/sdk'

const client = new ISnapClient({ apiKey: process.env.ISNAP_API_KEY ?? '', maxRetries: 2 })
const webhookId = 'wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6'
const since = new Date(Date.now() - 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000).toISOString()

// Newest first: the first record seen for an event is its latest delivery.
const latest = new Map<string, { id: string; status: string }>()
for await (const delivery of client.webhooks.deliveries(webhookId, { since, limit: 100 })) {
  if (delivery.event_id && !latest.has(delivery.event_id)) latest.set(delivery.event_id, delivery)
}

for (const delivery of latest.values()) {
  if (delivery.status !== 'failed') continue
  try {
    const replay = await client.webhooks.replay(webhookId, delivery.id)
    console.log(`replayed ${delivery.id} as ${replay.delivery_id}`)
  } catch (err) {
    // The record was deleted at the end of its retention: nothing to replay.
    if (err instanceof APIError && err.code === 'not_found') continue
    throw err
  }
}
```

```python Python
import os
import time
from datetime import datetime, timedelta, timezone

import requests

API = "https://api.isnap.ai"
HEADERS = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['ISNAP_API_KEY']}"}
WEBHOOK_ID = "wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6"


def get(path, params):
    """A read. Retries a 429 after Retry-After seconds, and a 500 or a 503 up to 2 times."""
    failures = 0
    while True:
        response = requests.get(f"{API}{path}", headers=HEADERS, params=params, timeout=30)
        if response.status_code == 429:
            time.sleep(int(response.headers.get("Retry-After", "60")))
            continue
        if response.status_code in (500, 503) and failures < 2:
            failures += 1
            time.sleep(2**failures)
            continue
        response.raise_for_status()
        return response.json()["data"]


since = (datetime.now(timezone.utc) - timedelta(hours=24)).strftime("%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ")
params = {"since": since, "limit": 100}

# Newest first: the first record seen for an event is its latest delivery.
latest = {}
while True:
    page = get(f"/v1/webhooks/{WEBHOOK_ID}/deliveries", params)
    for delivery in page["items"]:
        latest.setdefault(delivery["event_id"], delivery)
    if not page["has_more"]:
        break
    params["cursor"] = page["next_cursor"]

for delivery in latest.values():
    if delivery["status"] != "failed":
        continue
    url = f"{API}/v1/webhooks/{WEBHOOK_ID}/deliveries/{delivery['id']}/replay"
    for attempt in range(3):
        response = requests.post(url, headers=HEADERS, timeout=30)
        # 503 replay_enqueue_failed: the replay was not queued. Call again, up to 2 times.
        if response.status_code != 503 or attempt == 2:
            break
        time.sleep(2 ** (attempt + 1))
    if response.status_code == 404:
        continue  # The record was deleted at the end of its retention: nothing to replay.
    response.raise_for_status()
    print("replayed", delivery["id"], "as", response.json()["data"]["delivery_id"])
```

</CodeGroup>

A replay does not honor `Idempotency-Key`: a call sent twice creates two deliveries of the same
event. Your deduplication on `event_id` absorbs them.

## Secret rotation

[`POST /v1/webhooks/{id}/rotate-secret`](/api-reference/webhooks/rotate-webhook-secret) replaces the
signing secret and returns `200` with `id`, `secret` and `old_secret_expires_at`.

- **The rotation takes effect immediately.** Every request sent after it is signed with the new
  secret only: first attempts, retries of deliveries created before the rotation, replays and probes.
- **There is no overlap.** After a rotation iSnap never signs with the previous secret, and it never
  sends two signatures.
- **`old_secret_expires_at` is informational.** It is the time of the rotation plus 5 minutes, and
  it has no effect on what iSnap sends.
- **The new secret is returned once.** No other operation returns it.

Rotate in this order, so that no delivery is refused for long:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your receiver accept a list of secrets">
    Verify each request against every secret of the list, and accept it when one matches. With the
    SDK, pass an array as the third argument of `verifyWebhook`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rotate, and store the new secret">
    Call the operation once. Do not retry it blindly: it does not honor `Idempotency-Key`, and a
    second call replaces the secret again. If you lose the response, rotate again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the new secret to the list immediately">
    Between the rotation and this step, your receiver cannot verify the requests iSnap sends. Return
    a status outside `2xx` for a signature you cannot verify: iSnap retries the delivery on the
    [schedule](#retries).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the previous secret after old_secret_expires_at">
    Keeping the previous secret until then covers a request that iSnap signed just before the
    rotation and that is still in transit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The response contains the signing secret: run the command where its output is not logged, and store
the secret in your secret manager.

<CodeGroup>

```bash curl
curl -sS -X POST \
  "https://api.isnap.ai/v1/webhooks/wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6/rotate-secret" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ISNAP_API_KEY"
```

```typescript TypeScript
import { ISnapClient } from '@isnap/sdk'

const client = new ISnapClient({ apiKey: process.env.ISNAP_API_KEY ?? '', maxRetries: 2 })

// maxRetries: 0, because a retry after a lost response would rotate the secret a second time.
const rotation = await client.webhooks.rotateSecret('wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6', { maxRetries: 0 })
console.log(`previous secret can be removed after ${rotation.old_secret_expires_at}`)
// Store rotation.secret in your secret manager now: no other call returns it.
```

```python Python
import os

import requests

API = "https://api.isnap.ai"
HEADERS = {"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['ISNAP_API_KEY']}"}

response = requests.post(
    f"{API}/v1/webhooks/wh_3Kp8Rn9Fh2Lm5Qx7Vw1Yz4Bc6/rotate-secret",
    headers=HEADERS,
    timeout=30,
)
response.raise_for_status()
rotation = response.json()["data"]
print("previous secret can be removed after", rotation["old_secret_expires_at"])
# Store rotation["secret"] in your secret manager now: no other call returns it.
```

## Delivery records

[`GET /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries`](/api-reference/webhooks/list-webhook-deliveries) lists the
delivery records of a subscription. A record describes one delivery: one event for one subscription.
It is updated at each attempt, so it shows the latest attempt, not a history of attempts.

| Field | Type | Nullable | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | string | No | The id of the delivery (`whd_`). |
| `webhook_id` | string | No | The id of the subscription (`wh_`). |
| `event_id` | string | No | The `event_id` of the event. |
| `event_type` | string | No | The event type. |
| `attempt_number` | integer | No | 0 before the first attempt, then the number of the latest attempt, from 1 to 7. It restarts at 1 when iSnap resends the delivery after a suspension or a stall. |
| `status` | string | No | `pending`, `success` or `failed`: see the table below. |
| `request_headers` | object | Yes | The headers of the latest attempt, with `webhook-signature` replaced by `[redacted]`. `null` before the first attempt. Its `X-iSnap-Webhook-Id` is an opaque identifier of the subscription, not its `wh_` id: do not rely on it. |
| `request_body_snippet` | string | Yes | The first 1,024 characters of the body of the latest attempt, recorded even when no request was sent because the URL was refused or its host did not resolve. `null` before the first attempt. |
| `response_status` | integer | Yes | The HTTP status of the latest attempt. `null` when no HTTP response was received: a network error, a timeout, a redirect, a refused URL, or no request sent. |
| `response_body_snippet` | string | Yes | The first 1,024 characters of the response of your endpoint. In four cases where no request was sent, a note written by iSnap: see below. `null` when there is nothing to show, which includes a delivery whose URL was refused or whose host did not resolve. |
| `delivered_at` | string | Yes | The time the delivery succeeded. `null` otherwise. |
| `next_retry_at` | string | Yes | The time of the next attempt while a retry is scheduled. `null` before the first attempt, once the delivery has ended, and while it waits for a suspension to end. |
| `replay_of` | string | Yes | The id of the delivery this one replays. `null` for an original delivery, and once the record of the original is deleted. |
| `created_at` | string | No | The time the delivery was created. |

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | Not sent, waiting for a retry, or waiting for a suspension to end. |
| `success` | A `2xx` response was received. |
| `failed` | The delivery ended without a `2xx` response. |

In four cases where no request was sent, `response_body_snippet` contains a note written by iSnap,
not a response of your endpoint:

| Note | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `not sent: subscription inactive` | The subscription was inactive when the attempt was due. |
| `abandoned: older than 24h` | iSnap found the delivery waiting or stalled more than 24 hours after its creation. |
| `abandoned: rescued 3 times` | iSnap resent the delivery 3 times after a suspension or a stall, and stopped. |
| A note that starts with `replay_enqueue_failed:` | The replay could not be queued: replay the delivery again. |

A delivery whose URL was refused, or whose host did not resolve, has no note: its
`response_body_snippet` is `null`.

The list takes these parameters:

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `limit` | 1 to 100. Default 20. |
| `cursor` | The `next_cursor` of the previous page. |
| `status` | `pending`, `success` or `failed`. |
| `event` | An exact event type, of 100 characters at most. A wildcard is not interpreted. |
| `since`, `until` | Inclusive bounds on `created_at`. A timestamp in ISO 8601, in UTC, ending with `Z`, such as `2026-10-08T12:00:00Z`. An offset such as `+02:00`, a missing `Z` or a date without a time returns `400 validation_failed`. |

Records are returned newest first. `has_more` says whether another page exists: `next_cursor` is
then a string, and `null` otherwise. A malformed cursor returns `400 invalid_cursor`. The cursor
does not carry the filters: send the same filters on every page. See
[Cursors](/pagination#cursors).

A probe creates no delivery record.

`request_body_snippet` can contain personal data: the beginning of an event, with message text and
handles. Do not copy delivery records into logs, tickets or other tools.

## Retention

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| A delivery record | Deleted at least 72 hours after its creation, whatever its status. The API does not bound the delay. A record is deleted earlier when you delete its subscription: see [Deleting a subscription](#deleting-a-subscription). |
| Replaying a delivery | While its record exists. |
| An event in the [events feed](/webhooks/pulling-events) | 72 hours after its last delivery, a replay included. |

During these periods, anyone who has an API key of your account, a line-scoped key included, can read
the full content of each event through the events feed, and the first 1,024 characters of each
request body and of each response of your endpoint through the delivery list.

## Deleting a subscription

[`DELETE /v1/webhooks/{id}`](/api-reference/webhooks/delete-webhook) returns `204` with no body.

<Warning>
  Deleting a subscription is permanent. Its secret and its delivery records are deleted with it,
  the requests not sent are dropped, and the events that only this subscription received leave the
  events feed. To stop deliveries and keep the records, set `active: false` instead.
</Warning>
