---
title: "Get the sending limits of a line"
description: "Returns the five sending counters of a line with their limits, the reply-lane budget and the time each full counter frees room."
openapi: "GET /v1/lines/{id}/quota"
---

- **Credential:** an API key of the owner of the line. See [Reading the limits and the queue](/guides/sending-limits#reading-the-limits-and-the-queue).
- **Line-scoped keys:** a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) gets `403 forbidden` unless the line is in its list, even when the line does not exist.
- **Events:** none.
- **Rolling windows:** every counter counts over a rolling window and none resets at midnight. See [The five limits](/guides/sending-limits#the-five-limits).
- **`*_frees_at`:** the time at which a counter drops back under its limit. `null` when the counter is under its limit, when the limit is 0, or when the time could not be computed: read `null` as "no known wait".
- **Limits in force:** the limits returned are the ones in force on the line. To change them, see [Configuration](/guides/managing-lines#configuration).

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 403 | `forbidden` | The request uses a line-scoped key, and the line is not in its list. |
| 404 | `not_found` | No line has this id, or the line is not yours. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
