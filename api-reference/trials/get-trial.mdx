---
title: "Get a trial"
description: "Returns one trial that your account owns, with its metadata."
openapi: "GET /v1/trial/{trial_id}"
---

- **Credential:** an API key of the account that owns the trial. A trial key returns `401 unauthorized`.
- **Line-scoped keys:** the list of lines of a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) is not applied.
- **Events:** none.
- **Personal data:** `paired_handle`, the prospect's phone number or the email address of their Apple ID. Request it only when needed, do not copy it into logs, tickets or other tools, and refer to a prospect by an id of your own.
- **Status:** `status` can still read `pairing_pending` after the pairing window ended, until `GET /v1/trial/status` is called for the trial: see [Statuses](/guides/trials#statuses).

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 404 | `trial_not_found` | The id is unknown, or the trial belongs to another account. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
