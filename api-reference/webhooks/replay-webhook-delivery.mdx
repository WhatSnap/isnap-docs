---
title: "Replay a delivery"
description: "Creates a new delivery of the event of a finished delivery, to the current URL of the subscription."
openapi: "POST /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries/{delivery_id}/replay"
---

- **Credential:** an account API key. A trial key returns `401 unauthorized`.
- **Line-scoped keys:** the list of a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) is not
  applied here: the key replays any delivery of the account.
- **`Idempotency-Key`:** not honored. A call sent twice creates two deliveries of the same event, with
  the same `event_id`.
- **Events:** none. The operation delivers again the event of the delivery, with its `event_id` and
  its content unchanged: it creates no new event.
- **Guide:** [Replays](/webhooks/delivery#replays). Only a delivery whose `status` is `success`
  or `failed` can be replayed, while its record exists. The replay goes to the URL the
  subscription has now, signed with the secret it has now.

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `validation_failed` | `id` is not `wh_` followed by 25 letters or digits, or `delivery_id` is not `whd_` followed by 25 letters or digits. |
| 404 | `not_found` | Your account has no subscription with this id, or the subscription has no delivery with this id: the id is unknown, the record was deleted at the end of its retention, or the delivery belongs to another subscription. |
| 422 | `delivery_not_terminal` | The delivery is still `pending`. |
| 503 | `replay_enqueue_failed` | iSnap could not queue the replay. The new delivery exists with status `failed`: call again. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
