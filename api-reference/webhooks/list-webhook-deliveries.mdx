---
title: "List the deliveries of a webhook subscription"
description: "Lists the delivery records of a webhook subscription, newest first, with a cursor."
openapi: "GET /v1/webhooks/{id}/deliveries"
---

- **Credential:** an account API key. A trial key returns `401 unauthorized`.
- **Line-scoped keys:** the list of a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) is not
  applied here: the key reads the deliveries of the events of every line of the account.
- **`Idempotency-Key`:** not needed: the operation changes nothing. See
  [Retrying safely](/idempotency#retrying-safely).
- **Events:** none.
- **Personal data:** `request_body_snippet` contains the beginning of an event, with message text and
  handles, and `response_body_snippet` the beginning of the response of your endpoint. Request it
  only when needed, do not copy it into logs, tickets or other tools, and refer to a contact by an id
  of your own.
- **Guide:** [Delivery records](/webhooks/delivery#delivery-records) explains each field, and
  [Retention](/webhooks/delivery#retention) says how long a record exists. A record describes one
  delivery and shows its latest attempt.

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `validation_failed` | `id` is not `wh_` followed by 25 letters or digits. `limit` is outside 1 to 100. `status` is not `pending`, `success` or `failed`. `event` is longer than 100 characters. `since` or `until` is not a timestamp in ISO 8601, in UTC, ending with `Z`. |
| 400 | `invalid_cursor` | `cursor` is not a cursor this operation returned. |
| 404 | `not_found` | Your account has no subscription with this id. |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
