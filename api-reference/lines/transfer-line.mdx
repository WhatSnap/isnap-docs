---
title: "Transfer a line to another account"
description: "Moves a line you own to another iSnap account, immediately and without the acceptance of that account."
openapi: "POST /v1/lines/{id}/transfer-ownership"
---

- **Credential:** an API key of the owner of the line. The receiving account is not asked to accept, and it can be charged immediately: read [Transfer](/guides/managing-lines#transfer) before you call this operation.
- **Line-scoped keys:** a [line-scoped key](/authentication#line-scoped-keys) gets `403 forbidden` unless the line is in its list.
- **Test keys:** a transfer sent with an `isnap_test_` account key moves the real line, with the same effect on billing: see [Test keys](/authentication#test-keys).
- **`Idempotency-Key`:** not honored. After a transfer, the line is not yours, and a second call returns `404 not_found`.
- **Events:** none, to either account. See [What events do not announce](/webhooks/event-catalog#what-events-do-not-announce).
- **`to_user_id`:** the UUID of the receiving account. The API has no operation that returns it. Ask [support@isnap.ai](mailto:support@isnap.ai) for the UUID of the receiving account.
- **Chats:** the line's chats go with it. The receiving account can then receive your inbound messages of those chats that were not marked read and the events about your earlier messages: export what you need first, see [A chat is never deleted](/guides/receiving-messages#a-chat-is-never-deleted).

## Errors

| Status | `error.code` | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | `invalid_recipient` | `to_user_id` is not an iSnap account. |
| 403 | `forbidden` | The request uses a line-scoped key, and the line is not in its list. |
| 404 | `not_found` | No line has this id, or you do not own the line. |
| 409 | `line_recipient_not_wholesale` | The line is a shared line, a test line or a dedicated pool line, and the receiving account is not a wholesale partner. |
| 409 | `android_rental_not_purchasable` | The line is an `active` `rental_android` line, and the receiving account is not a wholesale partner. |
| 409 | `recipient_no_payment_method` | The receiving account has no payment method on file. It saves a card in the iSnap dashboard: see [Accounts](/concepts#accounts). |
| 409 | `payment_failed` | The payment method of the receiving account was declined. Nothing moved. |
| 409 | `subscription_cancel_failed` | Your per-line subscription of the line could not be canceled, and the line did not move. A direct receiving account can already have been charged: see [Transfer](/guides/managing-lines#transfer). |
| 409 | `transfer_conflict` | The line left your account during the call, and it did not move. A direct receiving account can already have been charged, and your per-line subscription of the line, when it had one, is already canceled, with a prorated credit. See [Transfer](/guides/managing-lines#transfer). |
| 409 | `line_on_rented_macos_session`, `line_takes_no_macos_session` | The hosted macOS session of the line does not fit the next owner. Only iSnap can resolve it: write to [support@isnap.ai](mailto:support@isnap.ai) with the `trace_id` and `request_id` of the response. When that session changed during the call, these codes can follow the same billing steps as `transfer_conflict`, and a line that carried your per-line subscription then leaves your account. See [Transfer](/guides/managing-lines#transfer). |

The errors that every operation shares are in [Errors](/errors-and-rate-limits#errors).
